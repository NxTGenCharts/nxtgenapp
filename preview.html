What to check here

Click any sidebar item to move the active state — accent bar, gradient background, icon brightness, and the 1.015× scale. Hover the theme toggle, the New Trade button, and sidebar rows to see the elevation/glow/icon transitions. Click the sun/moon button to run the icon-flip animation and flip the page background so you can compare both themes.

AI Coach keeps its own purple glow on top of the shared active treatment — click it to confirm the two layer correctly instead of one silently overriding the other.

Resize the window narrower to see the date/time widget collapse to its compact "date • time" form around 1200px, and the New Trade button tighten its padding below 1024px.
